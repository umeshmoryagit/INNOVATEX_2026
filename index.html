About InnovateX

InnovateX 2026 is the flagship national tech and innovation festival hosted to bring together student developers, hardware builders, designers, and tech strategists under one roof. Over two days of high-octane hackathons, keynote sessions, and design Sprints, attendees will tackle real-world problems and showcase groundbreaking ideas.
